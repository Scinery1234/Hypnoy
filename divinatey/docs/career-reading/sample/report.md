### Week 1 · Find what’s in the way

*When you know what to do but can’t start, it’s usually not about willpower. Something makes the first step feel heavy.*

### Week 2 · Make it smaller

*Big goals stay vague. Small milestones give you something to schedule.*

- [ ] Write your goal as one sentence, like “Get a role as … by …”. Break it into three to five milestones, each something you could finish in a week or two.
- [ ] For the first milestone, book three 30-minute blocks in your calendar this week, and write what you’ll do in each.
- [ ] Add an “if–then” for the hardest moment: “If I feel like skipping, then I’ll do just 10 minutes.”

**For you.** Write the goal as "Have my first paid advisory piece of work by the end of March", not "start a consultancy".
